# Profile artwork

The profile hero shows a downward aerial view of sunlit white cumulus, open blue sky and subtle graphic stars. The image is the reviewed native Blender composition; it is conceptual artwork, not a photograph.

## Rendering and typography

The scene was rendered in Blender 4.3.2 with Cycles. It uses the author-provided quarter- and eighth-resolution cloud volumes. The native composition is 1600 × 1040; this repository publishes the reviewed 900 × 585 PNG without changing its pixels or metadata. Sunlight and native fill passes were combined in scene-linear radiance before the AgX display transform. A light high-frequency wavelet cleanup was applied before the editable SVG typography and star accents. No generative bitmap or photographic sky replacement was used.

Typography uses Nimbus Roman and Nimbus Sans from [URW Base35](https://github.com/ArtifexSoftware/urw-base35-fonts), under AGPL-3.0 with the font exception. Font binaries are not included in the repository hero.
